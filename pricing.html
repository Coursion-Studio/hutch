Why isn't all of it paid?

Because the line is drawn on competition, not on effort. Window tiling, menu-bar switches and link routing all have good free rivals — charging for them would just mean you install something else. And a launcher that won't launch anything is a locked app nobody opens twice.

What happens after the trial?

The free tools keep working exactly as they did. Nothing is taken away, nothing starts nagging — the paid features simply stop appearing.

Does it need an account?

No. Your licence key is signed, and Hutch verifies it on your Mac without asking anything. There's no login, and no server that can lock you out of software you paid for.

How many Macs?

Two. If you run out, release a Mac you no longer use from Settings → Licence and the seat comes straight back — or add more for $9 each.

What about Hutch 2.0?

Your licence covers every 1.x release, however many there are. 2.0 will be a paid upgrade, discounted for anyone who already owns a licence.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — see the refund policy. If Hutch isn't for you, say so and we'll refund it.

Do you take my data?

Hutch runs on your Mac. Anonymous crash reports and feature counts are on by default and switch off together in Settings → General → Privacy. The privacy policy spells out exactly what that includes.
